Made in Godot 4

## Headwaters
What it is:
* Headwaters is a game about rowing upstream while enemies try to sink your boat. You gotta stop rowing to aim and shoot, and the river also pushes you back down!! Kill enemies, collect scrap, and spend it at docks to make the next stretch a little less miserable.

## How to use
Itch demo link: https://mustardmustard.itch.io/breakwater

Or, you can run it yourself:
1. Open Godot 4.
2. Import the project's `project.godot` file.
3. Press Play (▶) at the top, or F5.

## How to Play
You start in a boat, pointing upstream. Hold W to row and use A/D to steer. Enemies arrive in waves, and you can either shoot them or try to get around them. (Going around them isnt advised because then you have less scrap for the higher waves)

Controls:
* **W:** Hold to row.
* **A / D:** Turn the boat. You can turn without rowing too but its a bit slower i think
* **RMB:** Hold to aim. 
* **LMB:** Shoot while aiming.
* **E:** Dock when you're close enough and moving slowly.
* **R:** Restart after sinking.

You start with one shot before a slow automatic reload. You can keep rowing while it reloads, so you don't have to sit there waiting!!!

## Gameplay loop
Row upstream, fight or dodge enemies, and collect the scrap they drop.

Docks appear about every 200 metres. Pull up, slow down, and press E to open the shop. Gameplay pauses while you're shopping. Buy some gear, repair your hull if you need to, and head back out.

Each dock can only be used once. Upgrades last for the current run, and the waves get harder as you go.

There isn't a finish line. Your score is the furthest upstream you reach before sinking. Drifting backwards doesn't erase your best distance.

## Features
* An endless, procedurally generated river with bends and changing widths.
* Rowing, steering, momentum, and a current that pushes you downstream.
* Aiming and shooting that force you to put the oars down.
* Drifters that chase the boat.
* Swimmers that repeatedly lunge at you.
* Bank gunners with warning lines and projectiles.
* Scrap bounties and pickups that fly towards the boat.
* One-use docks with shops and random upgrade offers.
* Faster reloads, extra rounds, stronger shots, and piercing.
* Boat upgrades for rowing, turning, and hull strength.
* Hull repairs.
* Hit flashes, cracks, death shards, oar movement, and a wake.
* Sound effects for combat, rowing, pickups, and shopping.
* A HUD, sinking screen, and restart.
